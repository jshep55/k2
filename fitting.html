Why Get Fit?

The right specs change everything.

Off-the-rack clubs are built for an average golfer who doesn't exist. Shaft flex, length, lie angle, loft and head design all change how the ball comes off the face.

A proper fitting can add distance, tighten dispersion and make the game feel easier. And because Kevin is also your coach, your fitting matches the swing you're building, not just the one you have today.

The Process

How a fitting works.

  1. Interview

    Your game, your goals, your current clubs and what you want to improve.

  2. Baseline

    Hit your current clubs on TrackMan to measure speed, launch, spin and dispersion.

  3. Test

    Try head and shaft combinations, comparing every option against your baseline.

  4. Dial In

    Fine-tune loft, lie, length and grip for the best numbers and feel.

  5. Order

    If you choose to buy, Kevin places your custom order. Clubs typically arrive in 7–10 business days.

FAQ

Fitting questions.

Do I have to buy clubs after a fitting?

No. There's no obligation to purchase clubs through K2. If you do, part of your fitting fee is credited toward your order: $100 for a full bag or putter fitting, $50 for an iron or driver and woods fitting.

How long until my clubs arrive?

Custom orders typically arrive in 7–10 business days.

Which putters can I be fit for?

Putter fittings are available for Scotty Cameron and L.A.B. Golf. The $200 putter fitting includes a $100 credit toward a new Scotty Cameron or L.A.B. Golf putter purchased through K2.

Am I good enough to get fit?

Yes. Golfers of every level benefit from clubs that fit. Beginners often see the biggest improvement.
