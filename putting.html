What Is TourPutt?

One slope. Every putt.

The TourPutt green is built on a constant 3% slope, the kind of slope you'll find on real greens. Putt from different spots around the hole and you get every putt on the course: straight uphill, straight downhill, left-to-right, right-to-left and everything in between.

Because the slope never changes, your practice is repeatable. Roll the same putt again and again until you own it, and see real progress from one session to the next.

  • Fixed 3% slope, like a real green
  • Uphill, downhill and every break from one green
  • Repeatable putts, so progress is measurable
The Pattern Test

Find your miss pattern.

The TourPutt Pattern Test puts you through a set of putts from positions all around the hole on the 3% slope, then shows you where and why you miss.

[PLACEHOLDER: Confirm how the Pattern Test works at K2: number of putts, how long it takes, and whether it's booked separately, done during a lesson, or available on a walk-in basis.]

  1. Putt

    Roll putts from set positions around the hole, so you face uphill, downhill and both directions of break.

  2. Measure

    Every putt is recorded so your tendencies show up clearly.

  3. Review

    Kevin walks you through your pattern: read, start line or speed.

  4. Fix

    You leave with drills targeted at the exact putts you miss most.
