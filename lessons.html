Your First Lesson

What to expect.

  1. Talk

    We start with your goals, your history with the game and what's frustrating you right now.

  2. Baseline

    You hit shots on TrackMan so Kevin can see your numbers: club path, face angle, strike and speed.

  3. Find the Cause

    Kevin pinpoints what's really causing your misses and you start fixing it together, with instant feedback.

  4. Plan

    You leave knowing what to work on, with drills to practice before your next visit.

The Technology

Coaching backed by data.

TrackMan

The launch monitor used on tour. Measures club and ball data so every change is backed by numbers.

TourPutt

A putting green set at a fixed 3% slope, so you can practice uphill, downhill and breaking putts just like on the course.

Impact Vision Simulator

Play real courses and practice in game-like conditions, year-round.
