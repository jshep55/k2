Coaching Approach

How Kevin coaches.

Every student follows the same path, whether you're a beginner or a tournament player.

  1. Assess

    Kevin starts by watching you swing and putt, with TrackMan and video capturing what's really happening.

  2. Prioritize

    He picks the one or two changes that will lower your scores the most, so you're not juggling 20 swing thoughts.

  3. Practice

    You leave every session with clear drills and a practice plan built around your schedule.

  4. Measure

    Progress is tracked with data, so you can see your improvement, not just feel it.
