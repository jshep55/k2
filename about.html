Kevin's Story

Hi, I'm Kevin.

Kevin's playing career took him to the highest level of professional golf. He earned his PGA TOUR card through Q-School, one of the most demanding qualifying events in sports, and went on to compete on the PGA TOUR.

For more than 10 years, Kevin has brought that tour-level knowledge to the lesson tee. He coaches beginners, weekend golfers and competitive juniors with the same goal: to understand exactly why the ball does what it does, and to make lasting improvements.

At K2 Golf Studio, Kevin brings everything together under one roof: private coaching, a TourPutt putting green, TrackMan club fitting and a place for members to practice year-round.

Coaching Philosophy

Every swing has a reason.

Kevin coaches on one principle: the golf swing is cause and effect. Your ball flight is the effect. Kevin's job is to find the cause.

No one hits bad shots for no reason. There is always something causing them, and Kevin will find it, explain it in plain terms and fix it at the source, instead of patching symptoms with quick tips.

  1. See the Effect

    Kevin watches your swing and ball flight to understand exactly what's happening.

  2. Find the Cause

    He traces the problem back to its source, the real reason the ball is going where it goes.

  3. Fix It

    You work on the cause, not the symptom, with clear drills you can take to the range.

  4. Make It Last

    Because the root cause is fixed, the improvement holds up on the course.
