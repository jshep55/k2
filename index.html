Meet Your Coach

Hi, I'm Kevin.

I'm a PGA Professional, and I built K2 Golf Studio so golfers in La Mirada have one place to get better: a coach who knows your game, the right technology, and clubs that actually fit.

Kevin earned his PGA TOUR card through Q-School and competed at the highest level of the game. For more than 10 years he has coached golfers of every level, and his approach is simple: every bad shot has a cause. Kevin finds it and fixes it.

  • Earned his PGA TOUR card through Q-School
  • More than 10 years of teaching experience
  • Club fitter for Titleist, Honma, Miura, Scotty Cameron and L.A.B. Golf
